Reducing pediatric morbidity and mortality where children live

We are clinicians, researchers and trainees who use large national datasets, multi-center collaboration, geospatial methods and community-engaged implementation science to generate evidence that changes practice, starting with RSV and pediatric respiratory illness.

Our approach

Our mission and values

We use whatever scientific methods are necessary to create rigorous, reproducible and transparent research. As a member of our research community, you'll find:

  • Large database and multi-center health services research
  • Prospective cohort and clinical trial design
  • Community-engaged, qualitative and implementation science methods
  • Geospatial methods that link where children live to how they do
  • Focus on pediatric respiratory and infectious disease outcomes
  • Commitment to translating findings into clinical practice
